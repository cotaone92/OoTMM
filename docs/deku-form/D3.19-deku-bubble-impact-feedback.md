# D3.19 — Deku bubble impact and expiry feedback

## Implementation
- Added a single `DekuBubble_Pop` path for normal actor impacts, exhausted rebounds and lifetime expiry.
- The pop uses OoT-native `EffectSsStone1_Spawn` particles and the OoT Deku impact sound `NA_SE_IT_DEKU`, positioned at the contact point when the collision element provides one.
- Shield reflection remains a non-terminal rebound while the rebound allowance remains; it does not trigger the pop effect.
- The helper kills the projectile after emitting feedback, leaving collider destruction to the actor's normal Destroy callback.

## Reference
Majora's Mask's Deku bubble arrow uses a burst particle and a dedicated vanish sound on terminal contact/expiry. This OoT port uses existing OoT effect/audio assets rather than importing MM-only sound banks or effects.

## Validation boundary
CI checks source compilation/typecheck and automated tests. Particle appearance, sound selection, exact collision contact position and in-game timing still require a generated ROM and gameplay test; those have not yet been performed.
