# D3.18 — Deku bubble flight, rebound and impact

## Implementation
- The projectile now starts with a slight upward velocity and low negative gravity, giving it a gently floating trajectory while retaining the charge-based horizontal speed.
- A collision with level geometry (wall, floor or ceiling) restores the previous position and rebounds the bubble. Walls reverse its yaw; floor and ceiling contacts invert vertical travel with a controlled impulse.
- Horizontal speed is reduced to 75% on each geometry rebound. The projectile is limited to two rebounds so it cannot ricochet indefinitely.
- A shield reflection (AT_BOUNCED) reverses horizontal and vertical direction and consumes one rebound. A normal actor hit pops the projectile; timeout and exhausted geometry rebounds also terminate it.
- The bounce counter is actor-local. No save data, Player layout or persistent state is changed.

## Reference and compatibility
The behavior follows the Majora's Mask Deku bubble projectile's broad interaction pattern: it travels, can be reflected by a defensive collision, and vanishes on ordinary impact or expiry. The OoT port keeps the existing cylinder collider and native OoT bubble display list; it does not claim bit-identical MM physics or collision geometry.

## Validation boundary
CI validates compilation/typecheck and automated tests only. The flight arc, rebound response, shield reflection and impact contact still require a generated ROM and in-game testing; those have not been performed.
