# D3.11 — Deku guard collision and damage path

## Implemented

- Holding R while the runtime form is Deku activates a guard state.
- The guard updates the existing OoT `Player.shieldQuad` using a world-space quad in front of the player's facing direction.
- The quad is registered in the AC collision context through `CollisionCheck_SetAC`.
- The existing OoT shield quad is initialized with hard AC collision flags (`AC_ON | AC_HARD | AC_TYPE_ENEMY`).
- The existing OoT damage update checks `shieldQuad.base.acFlags & AC_BOUNCED` before the body-cylinder damage path. A bounced hit follows the shield response and returns before normal body damage is processed.
- The Deku guard does not change the human shield equipment or register its quad while the player is in human form.
- Guard state is cleared when R is released, Deku form ends, or a blocked action state is entered.

## Known limitations

- Quad dimensions and offset are prototype values and need in-game tuning.
- The current implementation reuses OoT's initialized shield quad and its hard-collision response. It does not yet add a dedicated Deku shield visual.
- CI success verifies source integration and automated checks only; it is not evidence of a playable ROM or verified gameplay.
